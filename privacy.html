Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

Purpose

Personal Backup is a private backup utility operated solely by the owner of the connected Google account.

Google Drive access

The application requests the drive.file permission. It uses this access to create, verify, download and delete backup files available to the application. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts or calendars.

Data storage and use

Backup contents are encrypted before upload to Google Drive. Data is used only to create, maintain and restore the owner's backups. It is not sold, used for advertising or shared with other users. Google stores the uploaded files as the selected storage provider.

Credentials

Authorization credentials and encryption keys are stored privately on devices controlled by the owner. They are not published on this site.

Retention and deletion

Backup files are retained according to the owner's backup settings. The owner can delete these files from Google Drive and revoke the application's access through Google Account permissions. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing backup files.
